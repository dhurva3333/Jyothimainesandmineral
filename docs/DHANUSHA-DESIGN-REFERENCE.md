# Dhanusha birthday website — storyboard reference

The supplied storyboard image is the visual reference for this website. The site combines both storyboard concepts into a single 13-screen journey:

1. Welcome — moonlit fantasy title screen.
2. Ready? — greeting and invitation.
3. Birthday journey — illustrated path with jump links.
4. Our memories — five real photographs, individually viewable.
5. Little teasing — playful friendship notes.
6. All the good things — appreciation notes.
7. Surprises — interactive surprise cards.
8. Birthday cake — wish interaction.
9. A letter for you — personal letter.
10. The real you — kindness, support, humour and positivity.
11. More memories to create — photo mosaic and future-facing message.
12. Best friends? — friendship question and response.
13. Thank you — replay and share.

Visual direction: deep indigo/violet night sky, pink and lavender clouds, glowing butterflies, moonlight, champagne highlights, hand-lettered typography, scrapbook photo frames and parchment letter. The supplied reference is retained as the design source for future revisions.

Photos are stored individually under public/memories/memory-01.jpg through memory-05.jpg. Music is original synthesized ambient audio and starts only after the visitor taps the music control.
